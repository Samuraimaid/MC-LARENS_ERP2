# PHASE 4: CLEANUP SAFETY MATRIX

## Executive Classification

Every major folder/artifact is classified into one of 5 safety categories:

1. 🟢 **SAFE TO DELETE NOW** — Regenerable, unambiguously unused
2. 🟡 **SAFE AFTER BACKUP** — Unused but deserves archival
3. 🔵 **SAFE AFTER RUNTIME VALIDATION** — Needs build/Docker test
4. 🟠 **ARCHIVE INSTEAD** — Keep in /legacy_archive/ for archaeology
5. 🔴 **DO NOT TOUCH** — Critical for runtime/dev
